02 / THE APPROACH

Local evidence.
Physical reasoning.

A classical, CPU-first pipeline. No fixed list of arteries. No patient-specific edits.

01 / SEARCH

Start at the wall.

Crop around the parent. Build a millimetre distance shell. Propose connected bright structures at adaptive contrast levels.

02 / FOLLOW

Prove continuity.

Try a small cone of directions. Recenter successive CT cross-sections. Require a supported path of at least 5 mm.

03 / CHALLENGE

Reject weak evidence.

Check parent connection, contrast, separation, radius stability and multiscale tubular support. Suppress cropped ends.

03 / TOPOLOGY MATTERS

Count the openings.
Respect the anatomy.

Challenge rules define the target. A visually plausible branch is not enough.

02

Separate openings

Two nearby origins at the aortic wall count as two direct daughters.

01

Common trunk

One direct opening, even if it divides downstream. A daughter’s child is not another aortic daughter.

00

Cropped ends

Flat caps are not origins. Absent or out-of-coverage vessels must not be guessed.

≥5 mm visible contrast-filled lumen≥2 mm origin diameter*≤10 mm trace, or first bifurcation

*Minimum size is subject to the final dataset specification. Iliac division is outside the core task and may be scored separately. Current implementation: supported 5–10 mm traces; first-bifurcation stopping remains a limitation. Diagrams are schematic.
